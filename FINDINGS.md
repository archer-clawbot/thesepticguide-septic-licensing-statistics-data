# Key findings (snapshot Oct 21, 2026)

- 28,546 septic providers listed in 44 states + DC; 27,065 (94.8%) matched to an official state license/certification list; 22,183 have a phone number.
- 26 states publish a downloadable roster of septic license holders; 9 offer only one-at-a-time lookup; 15 have no public list found.
- 32 states license septic installers at the state level, 12 leave it to counties/towns, 6 credential only some septic trades.
- Most license-matched providers: North Carolina 4,522; Georgia 1,983; Connecticut 1,903; Virginia 1,688; Tennessee 1,610; South Carolina 1,468; New Hampshire 1,541 (100% of listed).
- Largest single license type: NC Certified Onsite Wastewater Installer (Level II), 2,956 providers.
- Counties with most listed providers: Fairfield CT 369, Litchfield CT 286, New Haven CT 274, Hartford CT 271.
- 15 jurisdictions have listed providers but zero license matches (incl. PA with 232 listed), because no statewide roster exists or was loaded.
